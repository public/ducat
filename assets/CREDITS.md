# Logo credits

The ducat logo (`logo.png`, `logo-64.png`, `favicon.ico`) is derived from a photograph of a gold ducat of
Doge Francesco Foscari of Venice (1423–1457), on display in a museum:

- **Photo:** Francesco Bini, via Wikimedia Commons:
  [Repubblica di venezia, francesco foscari, ducato in oro, con cristo entro mandorla, 1423-1457.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Repubblica_di_venezia,_francesco_foscari,_ducato_in_oro,_con_cristo_entro_mandorla,_1423-1457.jpg)
- **Licence:** [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- **Changes:** cropped to the coin, given a circular transparent mask, resized, and colour-quantised.

These image files are therefore licensed under **CC BY-SA 4.0**, not under the MIT licence that covers
ducat's code. The coin itself, struck in the 15th century, is in the public domain.
